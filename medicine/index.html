What gets in the way.

High ticket decisions take months

A patient weighing a five figure treatment researches for weeks. Most practices stop following up after two.

The ad platforms restrict what you can say

Google does not allow ads promoting many cell therapies or experimental treatments, so the pitch has to live somewhere else.

Inquiries go cold overnight

A form at 8:40 PM gets a call back the next afternoon. By then they have filled out two other forms.
