Ads that pass review.

Health advertising has rules other industries never see. One rejected ad can put a whole account at risk. Every ad we write goes through an Ad Policy Pre-Check before it goes live.

  • Education first campaigns, built around the questions patients actually ask.
  • No ad that implies a viewer has a health condition.
  • Landing pages that follow the same rules as the ad.
  • A claims file: every claim you use, mapped to its source.
Google allows ads for cell or gene therapies that are exclusively educational or informational in nature. Google Ads Policies, Healthcare and medicines. We build inside that line, then let your follow-up do the selling.
